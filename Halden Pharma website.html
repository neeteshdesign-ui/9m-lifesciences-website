We start with the patient and work backwards.

Halden Pharma was founded by two hospital pharmacists who were tired of treatments that worked in trials but were hard to take, hard to find or too expensive to keep taking.

Today our research centres in Pune, Basel and Boston work on medicines that are effective, easy to use and made to a standard we are happy to have inspected at any time.

We price for access, license selected products to local manufacturers, and publish the results of every trial we run, including the ones that did not go our way.

Where we work

Cardiovascular health

Blood pressure, cholesterol and heart-failure treatments, including once-daily combination tablets that cut down the number of pills a patient takes.

Diabetes and metabolism

Oral and injectable therapies for type 2 diabetes, plus dosing devices designed with patients who have limited hand strength or vision.

Respiratory care

Inhaled treatments for asthma and COPD, with dose counters and low-effort devices that suit children and older adults.

Hospital medicines

Injectables and infusions for infection and pain management, supplied to hospitals with reliable cold-chain delivery.
